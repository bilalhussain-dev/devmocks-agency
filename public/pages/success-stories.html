Our Journey to Success

From humble beginnings to achieving the $50K milestone - here's our story of persistence, growth, and success.

2022

Starting Local

Started with local clients in Pakistan, building websites and small applications. Our first project was a simple business website that earned us $300.

Early 2023

Fiverr Success

Expanded to Fiverr, focusing on Ruby on Rails and web development. Initial struggles with getting orders, but persistence led to our first 5-star reviews. Reached Level 1 Seller status within 3 months.

Mid 2023

Upwork Breakthrough

Ventured into Upwork, landing our first major project - a complex e-commerce platform. Successfully delivered multiple projects, maintaining a 100% job success score.

Late 2023

Team Expansion

Grew from a solo developer to a team of three, adding specialized skills in UI/UX design and mobile development. This allowed us to take on larger, more complex projects.

2024

$50K Milestone Achievement

Reached the significant milestone of $50,000 in revenue, with a diverse portfolio of over 100 successfully completed projects across multiple platforms and technologies.
